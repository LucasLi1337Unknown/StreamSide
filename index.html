Your setup recipe

RULE-BASED COACH

Bring StreamSide into OBS

  1. Open Studio and enable the camera. Finish your effects and captions.
  2. Open Clean output. This is a separate, uncluttered window.
  3. In OBS, add a Window Capture source and select that output window. Crop browser chrome if needed.
  4. Keep StreamSide open and the output window visible. Browser background throttling can reduce frame rate.
  5. Add your microphone in OBS separately. Caption recognition is a separate service; avoid double-routing the mic.

For transparent text overlays, use the timer overlay link with an OBS Browser Source. Camera output uses Window Capture; there is no virtual-camera driver.

OBS Window Capture guide ↗

Capabilities & privacy, without the mystery

Camera & face

Camera effects are rendered locally to canvas. Exposure is estimated from pixel brightness. Face landmarks guide framing and track accessories. No identity recognition or face reshaping. Digital correction cannot move your room lights.

Gestures

Google MediaPipe’s gesture model detects open palm, victory and thumbs up. Recognition is probabilistic. Models download on first use; GPU/CPU and browser support affect speed. Manual effect buttons always work.

Speech & translation

Live recognition uses browser Web Speech support and may process microphone audio remotely. On-device translation is browser-dependent. MyMemory is an opt-in, quota-limited online fallback for caption text. No unlimited translation promise.

Storage & sharing

Ideas, planner and transcript drafts are stored in your browser. No accounts or server-side transcript database. Share links contain a frozen copy of the text; anyone who has a link can read it. Stop buttons release camera/microphone tracks.

You control when camera, mic, recognition, model downloads and online translation start. Nothing goes live to a streaming platform from this app.
